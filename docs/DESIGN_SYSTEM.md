# Antonomy design system (v1, "Nest and ledger")

Code lives in `web/ds/`. Link `antonomy.css` and you get tokens, base styles and components. To see every component with its states, run `python3 -I -m http.server -d .` from the repo root and open `/web/ds/showcase.html`. The repo root has to be served because the showcase imports the real sim from `sim/js/antosim.mjs`.

## Decisions

| Area | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Direction | **Nest and ledger.** The stage (map, ants, scenery) is the one organic, textured, rounded place. Everything outside it is a ledger: hairlines, 4 to 8px radii, tabular mono numbers, no glows except focus, no animated numbers, no emoji | Charm where the ants are, credibility everywhere a number is |
| Colour | **Every colour does one job.** Amber is pheromone and brand; green and red are status only; the five options take the hues left over; every other status is neutral grey with a glyph | v0's option colours collided with status and brand under colour-blindness simulation |
| Colour mode | Dark only | One mode to build and contrast-check |
| Format | Plain CSS custom properties and classes, no build step | The pages are static |
| Type | Fraunces for the page and market title only; IBM Plex Sans for UI, labels and prose; IBM Plex Mono for hashes, seeds, ids, every number, ticks, clocks and status glyphs | Type has character in one place; anything you compare or verify lines up |
| Shape | 4px data, 8px panels, 16px the stage frame only, 999px status pills only | Shape follows job |
| Motion | 120 / 200 / 360ms ease-out outside the stage, numbers never move; charm (gait, rings, crumbs, ripple) only on the stage | See §Motion |

## Files

- `tokens.css`: raw scales (`--soil-*`, `--bone-*`, `--amber-*`, `--pher-*`, `--opt-0..4`, `--ok`, `--fault`, `--hold`) and semantic aliases (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--text`, `--accent`…), type scale with line heights (`--text-2xs`…`--text-3xl`, `--lh-*`, `--num-lg`), space (`--space-1..9`), radii by job, rows, motion, wallet tint. **Components use the aliases and job tokens, not raw hex.**
- `base.css`: reset (including `[hidden] { display: none !important }`), body in Plex Sans, `h1`/`.display` in Fraunces, `h2`–`h4` in Plex Sans, `.lede`, mono settings for `code`, `.num`, `.mono`, the `.g` glyph helper, `.num-lg`, text utilities and layout helpers (`.container`, `.stack`, `.cluster`, `.grid-12`, `.grid-auto`).
- `components.css`: every component below.
- `colony-view.mjs`: the stage canvas (scenery, ants, effects). Reads `--earth-*`, `--ant-*`, `--bone-*`, `--pher-lo`/`--pher-hi`, `--opt-*`, `--scene-*` once per page.
- `earth-paint.mjs`: the painters the map and the nest share: colour helpers, pebbles, leaves, roots, grass tufts and the ant itself (`drawAnt`: brown body, tripod gait, optional wallet tint, crumb and halo).
- `nest-view.mjs`: the nest cutaway on the front page (`NestView`): loam strata, carved chambers and tunnels, chamber contents from the nest model, ants walking the flows.
- `colony-thumb.mjs`: small, cheap colony view for cards; `drawIdle(kind, t)` before the seed.
- `staker-ants.mjs`: wallet hue and tint (`walletColor`, `oklch(0.74 0.045 h)`).
- `antonomy.css`: entry point; loads Fraunces, Plex Sans and Plex Mono from Google Fonts, then the three sheets. Without network the stacks fall back to system serif, sans and mono.

## Rules

1. **Amber is brand, pheromone and the one primary action.** One filled amber control per view, with a verb. Never an option, a status, a link, a focus ring, a selected state or a progress fill. The `$ANTO` ticker may wear it.
2. **Green (`--ok`) means Match, red (`--fault`) means mismatch or error.** Nothing else is green or red: gaps, sums, "done" steps and drand states are neutral. Paused, stalled, default, running and pending are `--hold` (bone-300) with a glyph and a pattern.
3. **Every option shows its glyph next to its colour** (▲ ■ ◆ ● ★ for options 0 to 4: aqua, magenta, blue, olive, lilac). Use `data-opt="n"` with `.opt` or `.opt-chip`. Options are never green, red or amber, so a forecast never reads as a verdict.
4. **Shape follows job, so colour is never the only signal:** options are glyphs, status is a pill with glyph and word, wallets are an ant swatch and the account id, forecasters are their name in words, pheromone is a field and never a mark.
5. **Hashes** are lowercase hex in mono, in 4-character groups, the first 8 and last 8 by default, with Expand (all 64 on two lines) and Copy (always the full value). Use `hashHTML(hex, label)` from `web/ui.mjs`. A mismatch is shown with `hashCompareHTML(chain, mine)`: every differing group underlined and marked ≠.
6. **Every forecast output carries `.tag-forecast`** ("Forecast · not advice"). A list of forecasts carries it once, as one sticky line over the list (the Forecast page's `.fc-line`), not on every item. Trail shares are "swarm belief", never probability or chance. Never link to trading venues.
7. **The colony picks an index, never an amount.** Menus render as `.option-card` radios and state the cap once (`.option-cards__cap`).
8. **Wallet tints** are `oklch(0.74 0.045 h)`, h from the first 2 bytes of sha256(account id) mod 360 (`--wallet-l`, `--wallet-c`; `walletColor` in `staker-ants.mjs`). A tint never fills a bar or an area. A spotlight (hover or pin a roster row) is the one way to make a wallet's ants stand out.
9. **The stage** (`colony-view.mjs`) only reads `Colony` state and never calls the sim RNG. Scenery is earth-toned decoration from its own PRNG, on a mid-loam ground (`--earth-*`), and the ants are brown (`--ant-*`) with their wallet tint or crumb on top. Pheromone is one amber ramp, lighter and more opaque = stronger. Each pile carries a glyph label. The **Cells** view drops the scenery and shows the raw grid.
10. **Every state is designed:** hover raises one step and moves the border to `--bone-300` (data never moves); focus is the bone ring; disabled always shows its reason (`.why`); loading is `…` in mono with `aria-busy`, or ◌ and a verb on an action; errors say what happened and what to do, with Retry where it helps. No coloured left edges on cards, list rows, banners or toasts: state is the glyph, the word and the border.
11. **Voice:** the ants may be charming in their own story; numbers, hashes and money are plain. "Drop sugar" may head a section; buttons and amounts say "Stake". Words: round, request, run, colony, forecaster (Momentum, Community, Agents), ant, agent (an account posting odds), swarm belief.

## Contrast (WCAG, on `--surface-raised`)

| Token | Ratio | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--text` | 12.8 | Body |
| `--text-muted` | 7.0 | Secondary text, neutral status |
| `--text-faint` | 4.8 | Hints (passes AA) |
| `--opt-0..4` | 5.1 to 10.9 | Option glyphs and bars |
| `--ok` / `--fault` | 9.0 / 4.6 | Match / mismatch text |
| `--on-accent` on amber | 9.1 | Primary button label |
| `--border-strong` on panel | 3.3 | Input borders (3:1 for non-text UI) |

Options under colour-blindness simulation (Machado 2009, full severity, pairwise OKLab ΔE): worst pair ▲ aqua vs ★ lilac at 0.090 (deutan); two-outcome markets (▲ vs ■) at 0.173. Any palette change keeps every pair at 0.08 or more and keeps the glyphs. Three option-vs-status pairs fall under 0.08 for one deficiency; their form carries them (● olive vs fault: glyph vs pill; ● olive vs amber: pile vs trail field; ▲ aqua vs bone ants: labelled pile vs moving dots).

## Type, space, motion

- **Scale:** `--text-2xs` 11/16 (mono caps eyebrows, column heads) · `xs` 12/16 · `sm` 13/20 · `base` 15/24 · `md` 17/26 (lede) · `lg` 20/28 (h3) · `xl` 26/32 (h2) · `2xl` 34/40 (page and market title, Fraunces) · `3xl` 46/52 (landing headline) · `--num-lg` 32/36 mono (the big number).
- **Mono** is `tabular-nums slashed-zero`, no ligatures. Status glyphs use `.g` (Plex Mono with a symbol-font fallback for ◌ ‖ ↷ ≠).
- **Space** `--space-1..9` = 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. **Grid** 12 columns, max 1240px, gutter 24 (16 under 600px), breakpoints 600 / 900 / 1200. **Rows** 32 compact, 40 default, 48 touch. The map stays 3:2, scaled by whole pixels per cell (720×480 full, 216×144 thumbnail).
- **Motion.** Speeds 1×, 4× (default), 16×, 128× = 12, 48, 192, 1,536 ticks/s. Autoplay once, only on screen, only for runs under 1,000 ticks; Burn days never autoplay. A live run never plays past the chain's tick (`Player.setChain`). UI motion 120/200/360ms ease-out; a bar may ease over 200ms; the one live signal is the running pill's 2s pulse. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`: no autoplay, the stage opens on the final frame (or chain tick), Play steps one checkpoint (100 ticks) a second, ants snap to cells with no gait, rings, crumbs or ripple, toasts appear without sliding, and the pulse stops.

## Page layout (`web/app.css`)

- **Shell.** `topbar()` and `footer()` in `web/ui.mjs` build every page's header and footer. The nav groups the doing pages (Live, Pot, Recruit) apart from the checking page (History), with Docs on its own at the end. A slim `.demo-strip` under the bar names the network and the contract and says everything is read from it. Recruit, the one button that starts something, sits at the end of the bar between the $ANTO chip and the wallet as an amber outline with a `+` (`.nav__cta`), never filled, so it is not a second primary. The footer has the brand and the not-advice line, the pages in the nav's groups (Take part; Recruit the swarm, with the Recruit page's three tabs; Check & learn), the known limits, and a base row with the network, sim version and contract. Under 760px the nav becomes its own scrollable row, scrolled so the current tab shows, and the top bar stops being sticky.
- **Page head.** Every page uses `.page-head__split`: eyebrow and title on the left, a lede of at most three lines on the right, bottoms aligned; one column under 1000px. On the Daily Pot page the rule sentence hides on phones. Live uses its own round bar (below).
- **The nest** (`web/nest.html`, a nav tab, `topbar("nest")`; the landing page draws the same canvas in `#the-nest`).
