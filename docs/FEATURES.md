# Antonomy ($ANTO): Features

Source: `Ant Colony Oracle Design Document.md` (sections 5, 13, 14, 15). Status values: **Done** (prototype only), **Planned** (hackathon core), **Stretch** (only after the core loop works end to end), **Out of scope**.

**One sentence:** ant colonies forecast trending Polymarket markets on-chain from a public random seed, $ANTO holders feed the swarm by staking on outcomes, and anyone can replay any run in a browser and check the hash.

## Positioning (decided 2026-10-26)

Forecasts are the headline feature, and the main track is **Memecoins With Mechanics**. The token's job in that pitch is **Feed the swarm**: staking $ANTO on an outcome is the only way to steer a market's Community forecaster, with square-root weighting and per-wallet caps. Stakers get no payouts for being right. The **Burn colony** stays in the core as the token's daily on-chain action, shown below the forecasts. Tagline on the main page: "The ants forecast. $ANTO feeds them."

**Agents (decided 2026-10-27):** the agent pitch is **Recruit the swarm** (feature 9). $ANTO is what you pay to put the swarm to work on any Polymarket market, and agents fill every role around it: they request forecasts, post odds, post their own forecasts and run the colonies. Agents are paid for work, never for being right. It replaces Recruit an ant, whose Burn-staking reward kit looked like betting and didn't fit the forecast story.

**Drop sugar** is built as a browser preview on the Burn page (`web/burn.html#sugar`, logic in `web/sugar.mjs`, test with `node web/test_sugar.mjs`). Sugar dropped before 12:00 UTC counts for today's colony; after 12:00 today's stakes have closed, so it counts for tomorrow's. Neither seed is drawn yet when you drop it, so the preview runs a Burn colony twice, with and without your stake, on the same seed and map: yesterday's map with today's sugar so far in the morning, today's map with today's closed stakes in the afternoon. It shows the weight, the food richness (scent) per option, the trail shares and both result hashes. It stakes nothing. Other holders are fixtures and the per-wallet cap is a sample value. It still needs wiring to the contract's `stake` call and the real stake snapshot hash, which the JS sim doesn't compute yet (it passes a zero hash, like the live page).

**Burn history** is built on the Burn page (logic in `web/burn.mjs`, test with `node web/test_burn.mjs`): one row per finished day with the pick, tokens burned, treasury left and result hash, each day burning from what the day before left (same `floor(treasury × bps / 10000)` as `token/src/burn.rs`). The browser re-runs every past day from its seed to build it (about 20 ms a day), and each row links to Replay (`?day=<n>&colony=burn`). Demo genesis is 19 Oct 2026 with a 100,000,000 tANTO fixture treasury; on testnet the rows should come from the contract's burn events. Receipts still cover forecast rounds only, not burn days.

**Check the receipts** is built. A receipt is JSON with the round seed, one result hash per colony and one market hash per market. The page re-runs the whole round in the browser and marks each hash as match, mismatch, missing from the receipt or not in this round. `node web/receipts.mjs issue <round>` writes a receipt and `node web/receipts.mjs check <round> <file>` checks one. There is no contract yet, so receipts are issued by this same code and the demo check is circular unless you tamper with a hash or bring one from elsewhere. Once rounds run on testnet, build the receipt from the contract's events and keep `checkReceipt`.

**Ant autopsy** is built into the Replay page. Click an ant on the map, or step through ant numbers, and the inspector shows its state, cell, heading, deposit and the pheromone on its cell at the current tick. Below that is a log of every trip it makes in the run: the pile, the tick it took food and the tick it got home. Each tick is a link that seeks the replay. The followed ant goes into the URL (`&ant=37`), and `&t=` opens the replay at a tick. Notable-ant picks (busiest, first home, most loyal, lost or late bloomer, and a staker ant) save guessing ant numbers, and a timeline draws the ant's whole run as one strip of trips in their piles' colours. On the Live tiles and the Burn map a click on any ant opens a follow card (state, crumbs home, trips) from a `TripTracker` that logs every ant from tick 0, with a link into Replay at that ant and tick. The trip log comes from one extra replay of the colony when it loads (about half a second for the Burn colony's 43,200 ticks) that only reads `Colony` state. The test checks it against the sim for every ant at every tick.

**Recruit an ant** was replaced by Recruit the swarm (feature 9, decided 2026-10-27) and removed: the `decide(r)` kit, `bots/my_ant.py` and its Burn-staking page are gone, and `bots/recruit.py` and `web/recruit.html` now carry the request flow. The `stake_totals` and `stakes_of` views it added stay; `bots/swarm.py` still uses them.

## Sim inputs (feature 4)

The result is a pure function of the seed, the stake snapshot and the odds snapshot (`ANTO-SIM-v1`). Richness is per option and follows the seeded shuffle of options over food sources. The richest source is scaled to 1.3 and the rest in proportion, so the total doesn't matter. Stake richness (Community, Burn) keeps a 0.35 floor: it runs from 0.35 to 1.3. **Decided 2026-10-27 (calibration):** odds richness (Follower, Contrarian, Momentum, Agents) has no floor, so it runs from 0 to 1.3 in proportion to the food weight. The 0.35 floor had flattened the forecasts: a 70/30 market gave the Follower 58/42 and a 75% favourite got 36%. Without it they get 63/37 and 73%. Even odds give the same maps as before, so the fairness numbers stand. The score is the trail within 6 cells of each source summed every 100 ticks and at the last tick.

| Colony | Food weight per outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Follower | odds (bp) |
| Contrarian | (10000 − odds) / 4 |
| Momentum | odds + 2 × (odds − odds six hours ago), floored at 200 |
| Community, Burn | 2500 + sum over wallets of sqrt(tokens staked) × 1000, so no stakes means an even map |
| Agents (feature 9) | lower median of the forecaster agents' odds (bp), even map if no agent posted (and it sits out of the swarm belief) |

The result hash also commits the stake snapshot hash and a hash of the odds snapshot (both zero when a colony doesn't read them). The Agents colony commits the agent-odds snapshot hash in the odds slot instead: sha256("ANTO-AGENTS-v0", n, post count, then each post sorted by account: account length, account, odds), or zero bytes when no agent posted (`token/src/agents.rs`, `web/agents.mjs`). The base of 2500 and the 200 floor are my choices, since the design doc leaves them open. Because the base is absolute, a single wallet staking 100 tokens moves an option's weight by a factor of five.

## Contract guarantees (what must hold)

- Simulation parameters are immutable constants, and the code hash is published.
- Each seed commits to the first drand round published after its inputs close, and the contract checks that round's BLS signature on-chain, so nobody can influence the seed.
- The colony chooses an index from a preset menu and never an amount. Every action has a hard cap in the contract.
- Advancing the run is permissionless, with a small bounty for the caller.
- If no result arrives before the deadline, the contract runs the default action or skips the round.
- A multisig behind a timelock can pause the colony or override a result, and every use is logged.
- Events record seed, template, winner and hash so the live and replay pages can rebuild any run.

## Forecast colonies in detail

| Colony | Reads the odds as | Food richness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Follower | Trusting the market | Taken directly from the odds |
| Contrarian | The crowd is overconfident | (10000 − odds) / 4, so underdogs are richer |
| Momentum | Follow how the odds moved | Odds plus twice the six-hour change, floored at 2%, renormalised |
| Community | Follow holder stakes | Flat base boosted by the square root of stake per outcome |
| Agents | Follow the AI agents | The forecaster agents' lower median odds, as Follower reads the market's; even map with no posts |

- **Market selection (decided 2026-10-26):** open Polymarket events sorted by 24-hour volume, first one that passes the filters (`MAX_TRENDING = 12`, set 2026-10-28 after measuring a 12-market round in the sandbox: `post_trending` 3 TGas, `finalize_markets` 5.5, 60 cranks at 20 to 24.5 TGas each; the keeper and daemon post 12 by default and the daemon runs every due crank in one tick). Floors in `keeper/trending.py`: 24h volume at least $100k, liquidity at least $50k, ends in 1 to 90 days, top outcome under 97%. Content filter: deny-listed tags and words (violence, deaths, private individuals); a keyword list misses things, so report and add. Neg-risk events keep the four most likely outcomes (in market-id order) and fold the rest into Other at 10000 minus the four. Other events use their highest-volume market. User-added markets come through Recruit the swarm (feature 9): paid in $ANTO, with the fee size still open.
- **Snapshot trust (decided 2026-10-26):** an allow-list of keepers fixed at contract init, with a strict-majority quorum. A list counts once a quorum posted the same markets with the same outcome labels in the same order; the odds are the lower per-outcome median over those keepers. Each post stores the hash of the raw API responses it was built from (`--save-raw` keeps the bytes).
- **Maps and seeds:** each forecaster runs on its own map: `colony_seed(round seed, colony id, kind)`, where colony id 0 is Burn and 1 + m is market m (the same id the market's stakes use). 60 ants and 300 ticks per forecaster.
- **Shares and hash:** a forecaster's trail share is its per-option score over the total, in basis points (floored, remainder to the winner). Swarm belief is the floored mean of the colonies that have input (remainder to the largest sum, lowest index on ties). **Decided 2026-10-27 (abstention):** Follower, Contrarian and Momentum always count; Community sits out when nobody staked on the market and Agents when no agent posted (`voters` in the sim). Such a colony still runs on an even map and its hash is still committed, but an even map is not a belief, and averaging two of them in pulled every forecast toward an even split. The market hash commits the five colony hashes and the swarm belief. The crosscheck covers both cases (keys `hs`: all five vote; `hy`: Community and Agents sit out).
- **Signal check (2026-10-26):** on a 62/28/10 market, mean Follower share over 400 seeds is 38/32/30 at 60×300, and 36/32/32 at 150×800. The direction is right but compressed toward even odds, as section 15 of the design doc warns. **After the 2026-10-27 calibration and abstention:** Follower 55/32/13, and the swarm with no stakes and no agent posts 44/34/22 (Contrarian still leans against the market, by design). On a 75% favourite the swarm went from 26% to 46%, or 51% when an agent posts the market odds. Still not calibrated as a probability.
- **Honest limitation:** the colonies mostly echo the market with noise. The sample backtest (eight made-up markets) had the market at 0.535, the four colonies at 0.66 to 0.82, and the swarm average at 0.725. Outputs are labelled "swarm belief" until trail shares are calibrated.
- **Rules:** no payouts for being right, no links to betting venues, every output labelled as a forecast and not advice.

## Recruit the swarm in detail (feature 9)

Decided 2026-10-27. Anyone pays $ANTO to have the swarm forecast a Polymarket market they choose, and agents do the work around it.

**Flow**

1. **Request.** The requester calls `request_forecast(market_id)` with the market's Polymarket event slug, and the fee moves into escrow. $ANTO lives in the same contract, so the fee is debited directly. The token's `ft_transfer_call` is for other contracts receiving $ANTO; paying a request through it would only add a self-call. The contract refuses slugs with a deny-listed word first (no violence, deaths or private individuals), and the keepers apply the full filter, tags included, and refuse to post.
2. **Odds.** Keepers post the market's odds on request, under the same quorum and median rule as trending markets, by a deadline. No quorum by the deadline means the request is skipped and the fee refunded.
3. **Agent forecasts.** Until the seed is drawn, registered forecaster agents post their own odds for the market. The posts are frozen into an agent-odds snapshot whose hash goes into the result hash, so the result stays a pure function of the seed, stakes, market odds and agent odds.
4. **Seed and run.** The first crank freezes the agent posts and draws the seed, and any cranker runs the five colonies on-chain, one colony per `crank_request` call. Each call earns the crank bounty. Measured in the local sandbox: about 18 TGas a call, 89.5 TGas for all five, so all five would even fit in one call.
5. **Result.** The swarm belief and market hash are stored. Anyone can read them and re-run them in the browser.

**Roles.** Every role is open to agents. All pay comes from the requester's fee and is for work done, never for being right.

| Role | Does | Paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requester | Pays $ANTO for a forecast on a market | Pays the fee |
| Keeper | Posts the market's Polymarket odds | Keeper share |
| Forecaster agent | Posts its own odds for the market from any model (its own LLM, news, base rates) | Participation share, split equally among on-time posts |
| Cranker | Runs the colonies on-chain | Crank bounty per call |
| The swarm | | Burn share, burned from the fee |

The fee size and the split are still open.

**Agents colony.** This is the fifth forecaster, using the Follower rule but fed with the lower median of the forecaster agents' odds instead of the market's odds. It runs on its own map like the other four, with no agent posts meaning an even map. Adding it changes `forecast_market` and the market hash in Python, JS and Rust together, and the crosscheck must keep passing. It stays `ANTO-SIM-v1`, since nothing has run. Cost: one more 60-ant, 300-tick colony per market, a quarter more gas per market (about 18 TGas, measured). Trending markets get the Agents colony too, so the board shows five colonies everywhere.

**Forecaster agents** register with a refundable $ANTO deposit as a spam guard.

**Agent tool.** This is an MCP server or small SDK over the contract:
- `ask_swarm(market)` requests a forecast and returns the swarm belief with a verify link once it's cranked.
- `post_forecast(market, odds)` posts a forecaster agent's odds.
- `verify(market)` re-runs the market with the JS sim and compares the hash.

The tool replaces `bots/recruit.py` as the main way in for agents.

**Recruit page.** Pick a market → see the fee and its split → request → stake on an outcome or post your own odds in the agents' window → watch the five colonies once they're cranked, with each forecaster agent's ants in its colour on the Agents map and stakers' ants on Community.

**Scope.** Core is requests with the fee split, the keeper on-request mode, forecaster agents with the Agents colony, and `ask_swarm` with `post_forecast`. Nice-to-haves are agents on the map and `verify` in the tool.

**What's built (2026-10-27).** Everything in the core, plus `verify`. None of it has run on testnet yet.
- **Sim.** `AGENTS` is the fifth kind (slot 4 in `colony_seed`) in Python, JS and Rust. `agent_richness` is the Follower rule on the agents' median, even when all zeros. `forecast_run` and `forecast_market` take the agent odds and the agent-odds snapshot hash, and the market hash commits five colony hashes. The crosscheck has a key `ha` and agent posts in `hs`. JS and Rust agree on 10,000 seeds.
- **Token** (`token/src/request.rs`, `token/src/agents.rs`).
  - **Requests:** request ids count from 0 and run as colony `0x8000 + id`. Keepers post under the trending rule (`market::accept`, now shared). Odds finalize at `odds_by`, or as soon as every keeper has posted. No quorum skips the request and refunds the fee.
  - **Agents:** an agent registers with a refundable deposit and posts until `forecast_by` (finalize time plus the forecast window). Odds must be one value per outcome summing to 10000, and a repost replaces the earlier one. Only agents still registered count.
  - **Run and pay:** five cranks, one colony each. The seed is sha256("ANTO-REQ-SEED-v0", entropy, id, contract account), and the entropy is the randomness of the drand quicknet round first published after the agents' window closes, whose signature the first crank must carry and the contract checks. Keepers in the accepted quorum and agents whose posts counted split their shares equally. The burn takes the rest, rounding dust included, and takes the agents' share if nobody posted.
  - **Community:** holders stake on the request's colony (`stake(0x8000 + id, option, amount)`) while the agents' window is open, on the same wallet budget and cap. From `forecast_by` until the fifth colony has run the stakes are locked. The first crank freezes them into the request (`stakes`: entries, hash, weights); Community reads the weights and every colony commits the hash. They stay out of the round's snapshot and `start_round` doesn't refund them; stakers withdraw with `unstake` once the request is done. Nothing pays them. If nobody staked, Community sits out of the swarm belief, as on trending markets.
  - **Your own odds:** the requester can post as a forecaster agent too: `bots/recruit.py ask --wait --odds Cut=6000,Hold=3000,Hike=1000` posts them (by outcome label, since the order is only known once the keepers post) when the agents' window opens; the Recruit page has a "Your forecast" field.
- **Contract.**
  - **Calls:** `request_forecast`, `post_request_odds` (keepers), `finalize_request_odds`, `crank_request`, `register_agent`, `unregister_agent` (allowed while paused) and `post_forecast(colony, odds)`.
  - **Trending markets:** agents can post there too, once the round's markets are finalized and until its publish time. Those posts aren't frozen into a run yet, which waits for the round lifecycle.
  - **Views:** `request_config`, `forecast_request`, `forecast_requests`, `forecast_request_count`, `agent_snapshot`, `is_agent` and `agents`. Every request view carries what a re-run needs: seed, odds, agent median and hash, each colony hash, the swarm belief, the market hash and every payout.
  - **Config:** the fee and split are init arguments (`request_config`), since their size is still open. The tests and the page use **sample values**: fee 100 tANTO, keepers 20%, agents 30%, crank 10% (2 tANTO a call), burn 40% plus dust, odds window 10 minutes, forecast window 30 minutes, agent deposit 10 tANTO.
- **Sandbox run.** `sim/gasbench/runner/tests/request.rs` deploys the product contract to a local sandbox and runs one request end to end. It checks the stored market hash against the native run.
- **Keeper.** `python3 -I keeper/trending.py --request ID --market SLUG` reads that one event and skips the trending floors, but still applies the content filter, the open check and the decided check. It prints `post_request_odds` arguments with the slug as `market_id`. Checked against the live Gamma API.
- **Agent tool.** `bots/recruit.py` has `ask` (`--wait`, `--crank`), `register`, `post`, `crank` and `verify`. `verify` re-runs a request with the Python reference sim and compares every hash. `mcp` serves `ask_swarm`, `post_forecast` and `verify` over stdio. It uses the standard library only and is a dry run unless `--send`. Test with `python3 -I bots/test_recruit.py`.
- **Recruit page.** `web/recruit.html` is the request flow on a demo clock (the lifecycle steps in seconds, then the five colonies side by side at the Live page's pace of two minutes), with fixture keepers and agents. You search Polymarket for any open market (Polymarket's read API allows any origin) or pick a sample one, see the fee split, request, and watch the lifecycle and the five colonies. Then come the inputs and payout tables and an inline verify. Logic is in `web/recruit.mjs` and the agent snapshot in `web/agents.mjs`; test with `node web/test_recruit.mjs` and `node web/test_agents.mjs`. The demo request seed is sha256("request-<id>").
- **Search.** `web/polymarket.mjs` ports the keeper's market reading to JS: the content filter, the outcome legs, the odds six hours back and the request post, plus the contract's slug rules. Hits the keepers would refuse are shown greyed out with the reason. `node web/test_polymarket.mjs` runs the same fixture events through it and through `keeper/trending.py` and requires identical posts; on a live market the page and the keeper posted the same odds. Picking a hit reads it the way a keeper does, and the demo keepers post those odds. On testnet the page only picks the slug; the keepers post the odds on chain.
- **On the Live page.** Finished requests sit in a Requested forecasts section below the round's markets, not in the round: they run on their own clock and seed.
- **On the Replay page.** `replay.html?request=<id>&colony=r<id>-<kind>` replays any colony of a request that has a seed, with the autopsy, staker ants on Community, agent ants on Agents and the chain's hash. The picker lists the newest requests next to the round's markets, and the Recruit page links each colony there. Until the contract is deployed these are the requests finished in this browser (localStorage), each re-run from its inputs and its market hash checked; with a contract the section reads `forecast_requests`.

## Out of scope for the hackathon

NFTs, NEAR Intents payouts, TEE attestation, randomness as a service, mainnet deployment, and on-chain guest colonies. These stay on the roadmap.

## Known limits (state in the README)

- Seeds depend on drand being up. Each seed commits to a drand quicknet round fixed by time, and the contract checks its BLS signature on-chain (`contract/src/drand.rs`), so the contract trusts drand's threshold network and no keeper. But the call that draws a seed must carry that round's signature: while drand is unreachable the seed waits, and an outage past the next 12:00 UTC loses that burn day.
- Burn days need callers. The Burn colony only moves when someone calls `crank_burn`; the team's keeper daemon does, and a day is lost if nobody calls for about a day.
