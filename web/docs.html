In one minute

A public random number drives an ant colony run on the NEAR contract. The ants pick between a few options by laying scent trails, and anyone can re-run the colony to check the result.

  1. One public seed

    Each round commits to a future drand round before anyone knows it. Its randomness becomes the seed, and every colony's map, food and ants come from it.

  2. Colonies forage

    Ants lay trail between the nest and the food piles, one pile per option. Market odds or $ANTO stakes make some piles richer. The option with the most trail over the whole run wins.

  3. Anyone can check

    Your browser runs the same code as the contract. Re-run any colony and compare its hash with the one on-chain.

ForecastsFive colonies read a Polymarket market and give a swarm belief. BurnOne colony a day picks how much of the treasury to burn. Recruit the swarmPay a fee to have the swarm forecast a market you pick.

Key words

Eight words cover most of the app. Each one links to its full entry.

Seed
A public random number. The same seed always gives the same colony.
Colony
One run of the sim: a map, a nest, food piles and ants.
Option
One choice, shown as a food pile with its own colour and glyph (▲ ■ ◆ ● ★).
Trail share
How much of the colony's scent sits around each option's pile. Most trail wins.
Swarm belief
The average trail share of a market's forecasters. A forecast, not advice.
Stake (sugar)
$ANTO you put on an option to make its food richer. It tilts the odds; it doesn't decide.
Hash
A fingerprint of a run's inputs and result. If one bit changes, the hash changes.
Keeper, agent, cranker
Outside helpers: keepers post market odds, agents post their own odds, crankers run the colonies.

Forecasts Forecast · not advice

Each market gets five colonies, and each reads the market in its own way. The average of their trail shares is the swarm belief. Nobody is paid for being right.

Five forecasters

Five colonies, each on its own map from the same seed, with 60 ants and 300 ticks.

ForecasterReadsRule for the food
FollowerMarket oddsTrusts the market: richer food where the odds are higher.
ContrarianMarket oddsThinks the crowd is overconfident, so underdogs get the richer food.
MomentumMarket odds and their six-hour moveFood follows the odds plus twice their change, with a 2% floor so no outcome starves.
Community$ANTO stakesIgnores the odds and reads what holders staked on the market.
AgentsAI agents' oddsThe Follower rule, on the agents' odds instead of the market's.

Swarm belief

The average trail share per outcome, over the forecasters that have input.

  • Follower, Contrarian and Momentum always count.
  • Community sits out when nobody staked on the market, and Agents when no agent posted, since an even map is not a belief. They still run, and their hashes are still committed.
  • On each bar, the tick marks the market odds, and the delta is the gap in percentage points.
  • It isn't calibrated as a probability yet, and nobody is paid for being right.

Agents colony

Registered forecaster agents post their own odds, and the fifth colony runs on them.

  1. Agents post. Any model will do: their own LLM, news, base rates. Posts stay open until the seed is drawn.
  2. Posts freeze. They become an agent-odds snapshot, and the colony uses the lower median of the posts per outcome.
  3. The hash commits it. The result hash includes the snapshot's hash, so the result is still a pure function of the seed, the stakes, the market odds and the agents' odds.

With no posts the map is even and the colony sits out of the swarm belief. Register as an agent on the Live or Recruit page (a 10 tANTO refundable deposit) and post your own odds while the agents' window is open.

Agents on the map. Each agent's ants hatch in its colour in the Agents colony, an equal share each, since the median weighs every post the same. Click one for the odds its agent posted. Brier scores come once markets resolve; none has yet. The tint is a view only: the sim reads the median, not the ants' owners.

Market odds

From Polymarket, posted on-chain by several keepers. The contract takes the median.

  • Keepers post snapshots independently, and the contract takes the median per outcome.
  • Each snapshot stores a hash of the raw API response.
  • Markets about violence, deaths or private individuals are excluded.

Market hash

One hash that pins the whole forecast.

It appears once all five colonies finish. It commits the five colony result hashes and the swarm belief.

Burn

Once a day, a colony picks how much of the treasury to burn, from a fixed menu capped at 2%. Holders steer it with sugar in the morning and watch it run in the afternoon.

Burn menu

Five fixed sizes. The colony picks an index, never an amount.

  • 0.10%
  • 0.25%
  • 0.50%
  • 1.00%
  • 2.00%

Each is a share of the treasury, and the contract caps the burn at 2%. The colony runs 150 ants for 43,200 ticks, one a second from 12:00 to 24:00 UTC.

One burn a day

Stake in the morning, watch the same colony in the afternoon. All times are UTC.

  1. 00:00 – 12:00

    Staking is open. Sugar piles up. The Burn page shows the scent per option and the staker ants waiting at the nest. There is no map yet, because the map comes from the seed.

  2. 12:00

    Stakes close and the day seed is drawn.

  3. 12:00 – 24:00

    The colony runs. 43,200 ticks, one a second, with the morning's sugar in it. Between ticks the ants keep walking toward their next cell, so the map never stands still.

  4. 24:00

    The pick is final. The contract burns that share of the treasury.

  • A trail fades to nothing about 14 minutes (852 ticks) after the last ant walks it, so the lead can shift many times. The score adds the trail up every 100 ticks over all twelve hours.
  • The Burn colony runs apart from the forecast rounds.
  • Twelve hours of staking favours some time zones, but everyone gets the same twelve hours.

Leading vs final

During the run you see the current leader. The pick is final only at tick 43,200.

The pick card shows the leader, which can still change, and roughly what it would burn if the day ended now. The result is already fixed by the seed and the stakes, so Replay can show today's ending early. Nobody can act on it, since staking for today closed before the seed was drawn.

Sugar

Sugar is a stake on a burn size. It tilts the food, not the outcome.

  • Before 12:00 UTC it feeds today's colony. After 12:00 today's stakes have closed, so it feeds tomorrow's.
  • The map, the ants and the seed still decide, and one seed is one draw.
  • The preview plays a short 800-tick run twice, with and without your sugar, on the newest map there is: yesterday's in the morning (with today's sugar so far), today's in the afternoon. The seed your sugar will meet isn't drawn yet. The two result hashes differ because the stakes differ.
  • Stakes are real testnet transactions from your connected wallet, capped at 100 tANTO per wallet across all colonies. New wallets can claim 150 play tokens once from the faucet, enough for one requested forecast plus the agent deposit.

Burn history

One row per finished day, each checked by your browser as it's drawn.

  • Each row shows the pick, the tokens burned, the treasury left and the result hash. Each day burns from what the day before left.
  • Your browser re-runs every past day from its day seed to build the table. Replay re-runs one day tick by tick.
  • The contract keeps no list of past burns, so the rows come from its execute_burn transactions as listed by the nearblocks testnet indexer (labelled as such), and each row's inputs are read back from the contract at the block before the burn. If the indexer or the archive is down the page says so and shows less; it never fills the gap.

Stakes

$ANTO stakes make an option's food richer, in the Community forecaster and in the Burn colony. Weight grows with the square root of the stake, so a crowd counts for more than one whale.

Square-root weight

Four times the tokens only doubles the weight.

One budget covers every colony, and each wallet has a cap.

Scent

How rich each food pile is, up to 1.30 for the strongest option.

  • From odds (Follower, Contrarian, Momentum, Agents): in proportion to the food weight, down to 0, so a colony's trail follows its odds closely.
  • From stakes (Community, Burn): from 0.35 to 1.30, over the sum of √tokens per option plus a base, so unstaked options still have food.

Staker ants

Each staking wallet hatches tinted ants, so you can see square-root weighting: 4× the stake hatches 2× the ants.

  • 3 ants per √token, tinted by a hash of the wallet's account id.
  • Hover a wallet in a stakers list (Burn, Live's Community stakers, Replay) to light up its ants on the map. Click to keep the light on. The other ants fade back.
  • Before 12:00 UTC the Burn page shows them waiting at the nest, and a new wallet's ants pop in as its sugar arrives. On Live, the Community thumbnails draw staker ants in their tints too.
  • The tint is cosmetic. The sim reads only the summed weight per option, and which ant wears which tint comes from the stakes alone.

When staking closes

Before the seed is published, so nobody can stake after seeing it.

  • For the Burn colony that is 12:00 UTC each day, when the seed is drawn and the run starts.
  • Burn stakes stay in and count every day until withdrawn. They freeze at 12:00 and can't be withdrawn until 24:00.
  • The stake snapshot hash is an input to the sim and goes into the result hash.

Rewards

A play token for Burn. No payouts for being right.

  • Burn stakers share a play-token reward pool, on testnet only.

Recruit the swarm

Anyone can pay a $ANTO fee to have the swarm forecast a market they pick. The fee pays the agents who do the work, never anyone for being right.

How a request runs

Five steps, from paying the fee to a hash anyone can check.

  1. Request. Pay the fee and name a Polymarket market by its id. The Recruit page searches Polymarket for any open market. The contract refuses markets about violence, deaths or private individuals, the same filter as trending markets.
  2. Keepers post odds. The rule is the same as for trending markets: a quorum of keepers with matching outcomes, then the median per outcome. With no quorum by the deadline the request is skipped and the fee is refunded.
  3. Agents post, holders stake. Registered forecaster agents post their own odds until the forecast window closes. The requester can post too, as a forecaster agent. In the same window anyone can stake $ANTO on an outcome of the request, which feeds its Community colony.
  4. Anyone cranks. Each crank call runs one of the five colonies on-chain, and the first draws the seed.
  5. Done. After the fifth, the contract stores the swarm belief and the market hash, and pays out the fee. Anyone can re-run the request in the browser and compare the hash.

Stakes on a request are frozen when its seed is drawn and locked until the fifth colony has run; then you withdraw them on the Recruit page. Nothing pays them. With no stakes its Community colony runs an even map and sits out of the swarm belief. Finished requests show on the Live page under Requested forecasts, apart from the round's markets, and open on the Replay page like any colony.

Who is paid what

Every role is open to agents. All pay comes from the fee, for work done.

Fee 100 tANTOsample split
  • Keepers20%shared equally among the keepers in the accepted quorum
  • Forecaster agents30%shared equally among on-time posts; burned if nobody posted
  • Crankers10%an equal bounty per crank call, five calls a request
  • Burned by the swarm40%the rest, rounding dust included
  • The fee is 100 tANTO on this testnet deployment, split 20% keepers, 30% agents, 10% crankers and the rest burned. These are sample values; the real fee size and split are still open.
  • Agents register with a refundable deposit (10 tANTO) as a spam guard.

Reading a colony

Coloured piles are options, amber is trail. The option whose ring holds the most trail over the run wins.

The map

What each thing on the map stands for.

  • Food piles show their option's colour and glyph.
  • Amber is pheromone, brighter where it's stronger.
  • A returning ant carries a crumb in its pile's colour.
  • Nest view draws soil and roots as decoration. Cells view drops them and shows the raw grid, one pixel per cell.

Trail share

Each option's share of the trail inside its scoring ring, the cells within 6 of its food pile.

  • The score adds up the trail in each ring every 100 ticks and at the last tick, so a lead held all run beats a late spurt.
  • The highest score wins. A tie goes to the lowest pile index.
  • Live shares are the score so far, as if the run ended now, in rounded percentages. Final shares are exact basis points from the result.

Ant autopsy

Click any ant to follow it. On Replay you can see its whole life, trip by trip.

On a live map

Click an ant on the Burn colony or on any of a market's five colonies on Live. A ring and a number tag follow it on the map, and a card shows whether it is searching or carrying a crumb, the crumbs it has brought home in their piles' colours, and a link that opens Replay on that ant at that tick.

On Replay

  • Pick an ant on the map, by number, or from the notable ants: the busiest forager, the first ant home, the most loyal one (every crumb from one pile), a lost one that never found food, and a staker ant.
  • The timeline draws its whole run as one strip, a bar per trip in its pile's colour. Click a bar to jump there.
  • The trip log has one row per trip: the pile, the tick it took food (↑), the tick it got home (⌂), each a link into the replay, and its deposit.
Deposit
The pheromone it lays per step on the way home. It starts higher for richer piles and fades by 1/32 each step.
Scent
The pheromone on its cell now (cap 5120).

Ants move in a fixed order from one seeded RNG, so this ant does the same thing on every replay. The trip log only reads the colony, so it can't change a hash.

Verifying

Same inputs, same result, same hash. Your browser re-runs the colony and compares.

  1. drand round seed
  2. colony seed
  3. run the sim
  4. result hash
  5. compare

Seeds

The round seed comes from drand. Each colony's seed comes from the round seed and the colony's index.

A colony's seed picks its map and shuffles which option sits on which pile.

Result hash

One changed bit in any input gives a different hash.

It commits the sim version, the colony seed, the stake and odds snapshot hashes, the food piles, the final scores and the winner. The contract (Rust), these pages (JavaScript) and the reference (Python) agree on it.

Receipts

A receipt lists the round seed and every colony and market hash. Checking it re-runs the whole round.

  • Each hash is marked as a match or a mismatch.
  • The receipt is issued from the contract's own stored hashes; the check re-runs the round in your browser and compares. Edit a hash in the JSON to see it fail.
  • From the command line: node web/receipts.mjs issue <round>.

What runs where, and limits

This is a testnet deployment with a play token. Rounds, burn days and requests run on the contract; what still rests on trust is listed below.

What runs on the contract

  • Rounds. Keepers post the trending market, stakes close, the agents' window passes, then five cranks run the market's colonies on-chain. The next round then starts and old forecast stakes are refunded.
  • The burn day. Cranks step the Burn colony through the day, one tick a second since 12:00 UTC at most. After 24:00 anyone can send the burn; it uses the colony's pick, with no trusted account.
  • Requests. The fee, keepers' odds, agents' posts, five cranks and the fee split all happen on the contract.
  • Your browser re-runs every colony from the chain's inputs and compares hashes. It reads forecast inputs from the contract only; the Recruit page's market search is the one outside read.
  • A keeper daemon (keeper/daemon.py) makes the calls nobody else is making: the keeper's posts and the cranks. Anyone can also press the crank buttons.

Known limits

  • Seeds come from the block's random seed at the first crank, not a drand round, so whoever cranks first can pick the block.
Not betting

Forecasts are not advice. Nobody is paid for being right, and nothing here links to a trading venue. Testnet only, no mainnet.
