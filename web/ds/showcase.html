Sim & replay

This is the real simulation: sim/js/antosim.mjs running in your browser, the same integer code the crosscheck tests against Python. Ants leave the anthill, find a food pile, and carry a crumb home, laying amber pheromone as they go (brighter means stronger). The dashed ring around each pile is the area that gets scored. The scenery is decoration drawn from the seed and never touches the result. Switch to Cells to see the raw grid, and click any ant to follow it.
