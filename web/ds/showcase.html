Design system · Nest and ledger · v1

Autonomy, but ants.

The stage (map, ants, scenery) is the one place with organic shapes, gait and charm. Everything outside it is a ledger: hairlines, small radii, tabular mono numbers, no animated numbers, no emoji. Every colour does one job. See docs/REDESIGN_FOUNDATIONS.md and docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md.

Colour

Amber is pheromone and brand. Green and red are status only. The five options take the hues left over, and never green, red or amber, so a forecast never reads as a verdict. Every other status is neutral grey with a glyph. Colour is never the only signal.

Motion

Charm lives in the stage: six-leg gait, marching score rings, crumbs and the ripple on the winning pile. Outside it, 120, 200 and 360ms with ease-out, and numbers never move: no count-up, no scrambling hashes, no bounce on a match. The one live signal is the running pill's 2s pulse.

  • A bar may ease to a new value over 200ms. Nothing else outside the stage moves.

One budget covers every stake: the 100 $ANTO per-wallet cap counts Burn, market and request stakes together. A Burn stake stays locked until every day it counts for has paid, so it shows hatched.

Weight is the square root of your total on an option: 25 → 5, 100 → 10, so four times the stake gives twice the weight.

Over the cap is an inline error that says by how much and why, never a silent disabled button. Success goes to a toast.
