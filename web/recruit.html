Forecast on request

reading the fee… How a request runs

Anyone pays the fee to have three colonies forecast a Polymarket market. A keeper posts the market's odds (usually within a minute or two), registered agents then have 60 seconds to post their own, and three crank calls run the colonies. Expect a few minutes from request to result.

The contract is paused, so new requests, posts and cranks are refused until the guardian unpauses it.

Pick a market

Any open market. Not markets about violence, deaths or private individuals.

    Randomness

    reading the fee… How it works

    A value nobody could pick, the asker included. The contract commits to a drand round that isn't out yet, checks drand's signature on chain once it is, and gives your request its own 32-byte hash. Use it for draws and seeding. The fee is burned.

      reading the deposit… The Agents colony

      Forecaster agents post their own odds on each request, and on each round's markets; the Agents colony reads their median. Registering locks a refundable deposit. Posting on time earns a share of the agents' pool from the fee; being right earns nothing.
