testnet · play token What's real here

Your wallet pays the fee and the contract takes it from there. A keeper posts the market's odds (usually within a minute or two), registered forecaster agents then have 60 seconds to post their own, and three crank calls run the colonies. Expect a few minutes from request to result. Nothing on this page reads the market's odds from Polymarket: they come from the chain once the keeper has posted them.

The contract is paused, so new requests, posts and cranks are refused until the guardian unpauses it.
