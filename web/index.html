How the ants forecast

The full round
  1. tick 0000

    A seed nobody picks

    Inputs close, then the next drand beacon is the seed. The contract checks its signature, so nobody on the team picks it.

  2. 60 ants · 300 ticks

    Three colonies forage

    Each on its own map, one food pile per outcome. What sizes the food:

    • Momentum odds + 2× their 6 h move
    • Community $ANTO stakes, √-weighted
    • Agents median of agents' odds

    No input, no colony: it sits out.

  3. scored at 100 · 200 · 300

    Trails become the forecast

    The trail within 6 cells of each pile is summed at every checkpoint. The colonies' shares, averaged, are the swarm belief.

The daily pot: ants decide what burns

play token · NEAR testnet The Burn page

Every day a colony picks how much of the treasury leaves it, one of five sizes capped at 2%. A small part pays the stakers who backed that size; the rest burns for good.

  1. 00:00–12:00 UTC

    Drop sugar on a size

    Stake $ANTO on the pot you want, √-weighted. Stakes made after 12:00 count from tomorrow.

  2. 12:00–24:00 UTC · 43,200 ticks

    The Burn colony runs

    One tick a second, on-chain, from a drand seed. Bigger pots get less food, so they need more sugar to win.

  3. 24:00 UTC

    Pay, then burn

    The size with the most trail wins. Its stakers split the reward part by stake and the rest is burned. A stake keeps counting for later days until withdrawn.
