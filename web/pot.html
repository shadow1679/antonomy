    How the pick works

    Who decides: the colony, from a seed nobody picks. Stakes add food to a size's pile, so its trail grows; the handicap makes bigger pots harder to pick.

    Influence is by the square root of your stake, so four times the stake gives twice the weight. Your share, if your size is picked, is by your plain stake, split with everyone else on that size.

    A stake counts for every day until you withdraw it, and stays locked until every day it counts for has paid. One cap covers the pot, markets and requests together. When a stake counts
