What the colonies do

Forecast · not advice The details

They forecast what Polymarket is trading most right now. Every round, a trending market goes in, three colonies forage on it inside the contract, and a swarm belief comes out that anyone can re-run.

  1. A trending market

    A keeper (a trusted, allow-listed account) reads Polymarket's open markets by 24-hour volume and takes the first busy enough to matter: $100k traded in a day, $50k liquidity, ending in 1 to 90 days, no outcome above 97%. Markets about violence, deaths or private people are filtered out.

  2. Odds on-chain

    Several keepers post the market's odds independently. The contract takes the median per outcome and stores a hash of each raw API response, so the pages read odds from the chain, never from Polymarket.

  3. Three colonies forage

    After staking closes and the agents' window ends, anyone cranks the contract: the first call draws the round's seed (the block's random seed, a stand-in for the drand commit) and each call runs one colony. The seed draws a map for each colony. Every outcome is a food pile, its richness set by that colony's input. 60 ants lay pheromone for 300 ticks; the outcome holding the most trail over the run wins that colony.

  4. One swarm belief

    The colonies' trail shares are averaged into a swarm belief per outcome, shown next to the market odds. A market hash pins the three results, and your browser can re-run them and compare.

Three forecasters, one market

    The market comes from the contract (the keepers' pick), and its title is derived from the Polymarket event id the contract stores. To forecast any open market, recruit the swarm.
